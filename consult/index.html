Bridging Technology, Quality & Creative Execution

I am a Computer Science graduate from Pai Chai University in South Korea, where I also completed approximately one year of graduate-level study in Computer Graphics. My journey in web development began in 2016, building websites and digital applications for Korean technology companies.

My hands-on experience spans software engineering, QA testing, graphic design, video editing, content creation, and AI-driven workflow automation.
